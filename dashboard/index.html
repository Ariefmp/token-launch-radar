Every protocol DefiLlama tracks that still records no token, ranked by what its launch would be worth if its record stayed as it is — and what to do to farm it.

How the implied value is worked out

implied value = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × breadth

    Implied value is this entry’s own claim from the snapshot’s figures. It is not a prediction that a token will launch, or when. A protocol is said to record no token, never that it has none: DefiLlama’s record is not proof, and a protocol on the list may still carry a token its record does not name.
