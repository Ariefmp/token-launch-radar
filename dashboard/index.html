Token Radar

Every protocol DefiLlama tracks that still records no token, ranked by what its launch would be worth if its record stayed as it is — and what to do to farm it.

How the implied value is worked out

implied value = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × breadth × category × dominance × data quality

    The category leader — the largest pre-token protocol in its category by TVL, in a category of three or more — takes a further ×1.10, 2nd and 3rd take ×1.05: the leader is the category’s most likely next launch, and the snapshot itself carries the ranking that says so. A leader of a two-protocol category takes ×1.05 and a lone protocol ×1.00: leadership means beating peers, and with no peers there is no signal. A protocol whose 7-day change or listing date the snapshot does not carry takes ×0.95 for each missing figure: what the snapshot cannot measure is worth less than what it can. A protocol over two years old and still shrinking more than 10% in 7 days takes a further ×0.90 — an old protocol bleeding TVL has likely missed its launch window. Implied value is this entry’s own claim from the snapshot’s figures. It is not a prediction that a token will launch, or when. A protocol is said to record no token, never that it has none: DefiLlama’s record is not proof, and a protocol on the list may still carry a token its record does not name.
